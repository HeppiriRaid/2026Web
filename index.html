ABOUT ME

I am Koki Takamatsu, a senior at the School of the Art Institute of Chicago pursuing a bachelor's degree in fine arts in studio, with a focus on design and illustration.

As a Japanese who has spent half of my life in Japan and the other half in the United States, I draw inspiration from the diverse cultural experiences of both countries.

This website showcases my work from 2020 to 2026.

My calligraphy works have been recognized — winning an award at the 2023 Under-25 Mainichi National Calligraphy Competition, and the 2015 National High School Calligraphy Championship, granted by The Mainichi Newspapers Co.

Thank you for visiting.

BACK GROUND

From the age of five I cultivated discipline and focus through the practice of karate and hands-in-hand calligraphy — continuing my training year after year in Kanazawa, Japan.

Alongside martial arts I studied Shodō (Japanese calligraphy) under Master Yoshiyuki Fujii, whose mentorship connected me with a strong foundation in visual composition and the language of abstract shapes. Through the study of line, form and the balance of black and white space I developed a deep appreciation for restraint and intention.

GRAPHIC PAINT

CALLIGRAPHY

Calligraphy is, to me, a record of a single breath. The brush touches the paper once and there is no correction — only the honest trace of attention, balance and nerve.

I work primarily in cursive (sōsho) and semi-cursive styles, where legibility yields to rhythm and the line becomes pure movement. The pieces here were made with sumi ink on hand-made paper, sealed with my own carved hanko.
