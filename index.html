ABOUT ME

I am Koki Takamatsu, a senior at the School of the Art Institute of Chicago pursuing a bachelor’s degree in Fine Arts in Studio with a focus on design and Illustration.

As a Japanese who’s spent half of my life in Japan and the other half in the United States, I draw inspiration from the diverse cultural experiences of both countries.

This website showcases my works from 2020 to 2025.

BACK GROUND

From the age of ﬁve, I cultivated discipline and focus through the practice of Karate and Kendo in Japan. Continued my training even after relocating to Redmond, WA, at the age of 13. In high school, I achieved the rank of black belt in both curricula before choosing to dedicate my full attention to advancing my aesthetic and design skills.

Alongside martial arts, I studied Shodo (Japanese calligraphy) under Yoshiyasu Fujii, whose mentorship provided me with a strong foundation in visual composition and the language of abstract shapes. Through the study of line, form, and the balance of black and white space, I developed a deep appreciation for aesthetics that continues to inform my work as an artist.

SELECTED WORKS

GRAPHIC PAINT

Every painting app fought me in the same places, so I’m building the one that doesn’t — from scratch, down to the compositor. A Morph brush whose bristles reshape under tilt. GP-Mix, color that blends like paint instead of mud. And the core idea: every stroke is recorded data, so a soft, painterly mark stays editable after it’s made.

AI has been used for solely the help of writing codes.
